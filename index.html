08 — Organisational Compliance
May 2026 — continuous improvement plans, policy reviews and governance activities
Continuous Improvement Plans
CI 01 — Documentation Gaps in Client Records
Standards 1, 3 & 5
A review of client records identified inconsistencies and gaps in documentation of client interactions, discussions, decisions, service changes, incidents and complaints. In some cases, records lacked sufficient detail to enable executive and management teams to effectively review events, understand decision-making processes, or investigate complaints and service concerns.
CI 02 — Mandatory Training Completion — Newly Employed Staff
Standards 2, 4, 5 & 6
An informal audit identified that several newly employed staff have not completed mandatory online training modules within the required timeframe on AusMed. This creates a risk that staff may not have the necessary knowledge and competencies to deliver safe, effective, person-centred care and services.
